How to Spot a Phishing Email

Phishing tries to make you act before you think. No single sign proves an email is fake, so look for several together and always verify through a channel you already trust.

Before You Click checklist

    If you already clicked or replied

    1. Do not enter anything else. Close the page.
    2. If you typed a password, change it right away on the real site and anywhere you reused it.
    3. Turn on multi-factor authentication for the affected accounts.
    4. Tell your IT/security team and forward the email to them as an attachment.
    5. If money or card details were involved, contact your bank using a number you already know.

    Why a score, not a verdict?

    The dashboard reports a risk score with reasons. A real HR email can say “urgent”, and a careful attacker can avoid every red flag. Use the score to guide your judgment, not to replace it.
